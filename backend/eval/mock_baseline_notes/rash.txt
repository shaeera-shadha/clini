Presenting complaint:
Itchy erythematous rash on both forearms for 5 days, also on the neck. Not painful, worse at night. No facial involvement.

Triggers: Recently changed washing powder.

Past medical history: Childhood eczema. Hay fever.
Medications: Hydrocortisone 1 percent cream twice daily with some benefit. No new medications.

Examination:
Erythematous, slightly raised plaques on both forearms with excoriations. No vesicles, no weeping. Similar lesion on the neck. No burrows seen.

Diagnosis:
Allergic contact dermatitis secondary to the new washing powder.

Plan:
Stop the washing powder for 2 weeks. Emollients and a stronger topical steroid if not improving.
